# Support Boundary

LeadFlow Agent Pack is a self-serve lead triage template.

## Included

- Local demo workflow.
- Sample CSV leads.
- Sample JSONL output.
- Scoring and priority rules.
- Reply draft generator.
- Setup guide.
- Basic async setup questions.

## Not Included

- Managed sales service.
- Full CRM replacement.
- Automatic email sending.
- Private customer-data review.
- Guaranteed lead conversion.
- Guaranteed CRM integration.
- Live calls.

## Custom Work

Custom work is separate from the template purchase. Examples:

- Connect one real lead source such as a form export, Google Sheet, Airtable base, or CRM export.
- Rewrite scoring around one business's qualification rules.
- Create multiple reply draft styles.
- Add model-assisted classification.
- Send output to a CRM, Notion, Airtable, or another handoff tool.

## Correct Use

Use this pack to test a lightweight lead triage workflow before investing in a full CRM or sales automation setup.

Keep human review in the first version. Auto-send should only be added after the business has confirmed approval rules, tone, compliance boundaries, and opt-out handling.
